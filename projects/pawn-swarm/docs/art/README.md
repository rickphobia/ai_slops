# Art direction: dark fantasy horror

`style-sample.html` is the approved style sample (2026-10-02). Open it in a browser to see it.

- **White pawns:** hooded bone figures with stitched-shut mouths, hollow eye sockets with faint red pupils, a blood tear, cracked skull, torn robe tied with rope. Each type adds one strong prop or deformity (bomb, skewered eyeball on a spear, stitched-skin shield with a blinking eye, conjoined twin heads).
- **Black pieces:** scorched iron, charred bone and black ichor, ember eyes. Kept dark so the two sides read apart in a crowd. Knight: charred horse skull on a spine. Bishop: plague priest whose mitre opens into a toothed mouth. Rook: ruined tower with one huge eye. Queen: corpse bride with a crown of teeth. King: rotting monarch, crown fused to the skull, eyes opening on the robe.
- **Black types:** keep the piece's shape and add one strong change that shows the power (Stomper: iron-shod, cracks below; Hunter: three red eyes and a red trail; Priest: green-smoke censer; Sniper: stretched tall with a bone needle and a blue eye; Tower: iron bands and bars over the eye; Cannon: a smoking barrel in the window; Storm: lightning from the hair; Summoner: violet ritual circle with ghost knights).
- **Horror comes from small, wrong details:** stitches, eyes that move, things that drip or pulse. Blood is an accent, not a flood.
- **Board:** dark squares fading to black at the edges, old blood stains.
- **Type:** Cinzel for display, Crimson Pro for body text.

The sample draws each piece as an SVG in a 100×100 box. The game can render these to canvas images once at startup.
