# Art direction: grotesque dark fantasy

Two reference files, both approved on 2026-10-02. Open them in a browser.

- `grotesque-sample.html` — **the target look.** Body horror pushed hard: flayed skin and raw muscle, exposed ribs and organs, extra eyes and mouths, maggots, pooling blood. Shows plain pawn, twin pawn, spear pawn, knight and queen.
- `style-sample.html` — the earlier, milder pass. It covers every piece and black type, so use it for each piece's **concept** (bomb pawn's stitched bomb, shield pawn's skin shield with an eye, plague-priest bishop, eyed tower rook, rotting king, the 8 black types) and redraw it at the grotesque level.

## Rules

- **Drawn shapes, not realistic gore.** Flat vector drawings with dark outlines; horror comes from wrong anatomy and small moving details (eyes that look around or blink, pulsing hearts, dripping blood, crawling maggots, twitching limbs).
- **White pawns:** hooded bone figures, stitched mouths, hollow eyes with faint red pupils, torn robes tied with rope, then one grotesque feature per type (flayed face, hanging eye, fused twin heads with a shared third eye, a spear through its own body with eyeballs on the shaft, and so on).
- **Black pieces:** skinned, scorched or rotting versions of their chess shapes (skinned horse head with eyes down the neck, a queen whose face is a vertical mouth with four bone arms, a king with his crown grown into his skull).
- **Black types:** keep the piece's shape and add one strong change that shows the power (Stomper: iron-shod, cracks below; Hunter: three red eyes and a red trail; Priest: green-smoke censer; Sniper: stretched tall with a bone needle and a blue eye; Tower: iron bands and bars over the eye; Cannon: a smoking barrel in the window; Storm: lightning from the hair; Summoner: violet ritual circle with ghost knights).
- **Sides must read apart in a crowd.** White pawns are pale bone; black pieces stay mostly dark (iron, charred bone, ichor) with the red flesh as detail, so a red knight doesn't vanish into blood effects. Check this in a screenshot of a big fight.
- **Readable at game size.** The silhouette carries the piece; tiny details are a bonus. Check each drawing at about 48 px.
- **Board:** dark squares fading to black at the edges, old blood stains. **Type:** Cinzel for display, Crimson Pro for body text.

The samples draw each piece as SVG in a 100×100 box. The game can render these to canvas images once at startup.
