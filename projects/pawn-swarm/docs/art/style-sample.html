Pawn Swarm, dark fantasy style sample

Each piece is a small vector drawing, so it stays sharp at any size and can be swapped later for painted art. White pawns are stitched bone figures with hollow, glowing eyes; black pieces are scorched iron, bone and ichor. Each black type keeps its piece's shape and adds one strong change that shows its power. Horror comes from small, wrong details: stitches, eyes that move, things that drip.

White pawns

Plain pawn

A hooded, stitched-mouth bone figure with hollow eyes and a blood tear. Your money and your fodder.

Bomb pawn

Cradles a bomb wrapped in stitched leather, a skull painted on it, fuse already lit.

Twin pawn

One body, two heads. Twin-to-twin transfusion: a gaunt, pale head and a swollen, flushed one, a vessel pumping between their necks over a heart that shows through the robe.

Spear pawn

An eyeball skewered on the tip, blood running down the shaft.

Shield pawn

Its shield is stretched skin, stitched together, with an eye that still looks around and blinks.

Black pieces

Knight

A charred horse skull on an exposed spine, one ember eye, leaking black ichor.

Rook

A ruined tower with one huge eye in the window, chains hanging off it, blood seeping from the base.

Bishop

A plague priest whose mitre splits into a vertical mouth of teeth. Candle wax and blood run down its robe.

Queen

A corpse bride in a torn, bloodied veil, a crown of teeth, and a long-fingered hand reaching out.

King

A rotting monarch whose crown has grown into his skull, a cross of bone on top, eyes opening across his robe.

Black types (same piece, different power)

Stomper knight

Iron-shod and heavier, spiked chains on the neck, cracks under it where it lands.

Hunter knight

Lean and hound-like, three red eyes, a trail of red after it.

Priest bishop

Swings a censer of sickly green smoke that knits black pieces back together.

Sniper bishop

Stretched tall, with a long bone needle and one cold blue eye that never blinks.

Tower rook

Banded in iron plates and extra battlements, its eye shuttered behind bars.

Cannon rook

A cannon barrel pushes out of the window where the eye should be, smoking.

Storm queen

Her veil lifts in a wind that isn't there; pale lightning arcs from her hair.

Summoner queen

Stands in a violet ritual circle, ghost knights rising at its edge.
