Pawn Swarm

A grotesque chess swarm. Brotato meets How Many Dudes on a chessboard.

You start with one white pawn in the middle of the board. The black army lands around you in waves of pushes; red squares warn you where. Every black piece you kill drops more pawns, and they join the fight on the spot. Some kills also drop power-ups (coloured orbs): heal, haste, fury, freeze, bounty, reinforcements. From wave 3, black pieces come in special types with powers, marked by a coloured ring. The shop tells you which ones are coming next. Pawns are also your money: between waves, spend them to turn plain pawns into special types. Each type you own gives you a skill button (keys 1–9). Survive 10 waves and kill the king.
