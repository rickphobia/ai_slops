## Made for this project

- All sounds are synthesised in code (`src/adapters/placeholder_sounds.gd`): breathing, heartbeat, door and house creaks, wind, fridge hum.
- The grime texture is generated by Godot's noise (`src/adapters/look/grime.tres`).
- The PS1 shaders (`src/adapters/look/*.gdshader`) are our own.

## Tools

- [Godot Engine](https://godotengine.org) — MIT.
- [GUT](https://github.com/bitwes/Gut), vendored in `addons/gut/` — MIT.
