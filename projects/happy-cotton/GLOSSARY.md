# Happy Cotton

An endless farm game in the style of Hay Day that satirises the Chinese state's forced-labour system in Xinjiang's cotton fields. The player is a Uyghur Worker; the cheerful surface belongs to the state, the grim world underneath belongs to the people in it.

## Language

### People

**Worker**:
The Uyghur person the player controls, made to pick cotton for the state.
_Avoid_: player character, farmer, picker, hero

**Co-worker**:
A named Uyghur labourer on the Farm beside the Worker, with a story of their own.
_Avoid_: NPC, villager, neighbour

**Overseer**:
The official who runs the Farm day to day: a person under pressure from above, not a monster.
_Avoid_: boss, guard, villain

**Family**:
The Worker's relatives far away, present only through letters and calls.
_Avoid_: relatives, home (as a character)

**Mascot**:
The smiling cotton boll that speaks for The App.
_Avoid_: helper, guide, tutorial character

### The system

**Farm**:
The state-run cotton farm the Worker labours on. It grows, expands and earns; the Worker does not.
_Avoid_: my farm, the player's farm (a Plot is one bed of it, not the whole Farm)

**Plot**:
One bed of the Farm's field where the Worker plants and picks cotton. It is empty, growing or ripe.
_Avoid_: tile, cell, patch

**Stage**:
How far a Plot's cotton has grown: Seedling, Flowering, Boll, then Ripe, when it can be picked.
_Avoid_: level, phase

**Quota**:
The amount of cotton the state demands from the Worker in a period. It only ever rises.
_Avoid_: order, target, goal

**Shift**:
A stretch of the Worker's working time that runs only while the game is open, with one Quota due at its end.
_Avoid_: day, round, turn, period

**The App**:
The bright, state-issued "Happy Cotton" app laid over the world: the state's cheerful voice, never the Worker's.
_Avoid_: HUD, UI (when talking about the game's world rather than its code)

**Study Session**:
The punishment for a missed Quota or Negligence: a stretch of real time when the Worker is taken off the field. Each one in a row lasts longer, and a Co-worker shares the cost.
_Avoid_: timeout, penalty, re-education (The App never uses that word)

**Report**:
Telling the state about a Co-worker, rewarded with Labour Points. The App calls it "Neighbourhood Harmony".
_Avoid_: snitch, inform

**Withered**:
A crop that died because nobody tended it while the game was closed too long.
_Avoid_: dead, rotten, spoiled

**Negligence**:
The App's record of a Withered crop. It is punished more severely than a missed Quota.
_Avoid_: penalty, fine, mistake

**Farm Level**:
How far the Farm has grown. It belongs to the state, not the Worker, and each new level raises the Quota.
_Avoid_: player level, XP, rank

### The Worker's body

**Exhaustion**:
How worn down the Worker is. Work raises it, rest lowers it, but never below a floor that keeps creeping up.
_Avoid_: stamina, energy, tiredness, fatigue

### Small acts

**Quiet Act**:
A small, costly act of dignity the Worker can choose, such as helping a Co-worker's Quota, refusing to Report or keeping a hidden diary.
_Avoid_: resistance, rebellion, good deed

### What the Worker gets

**Labour Points**:
The only currency the Worker earns, a small fraction of the value of what they pick.
_Avoid_: coins, money, wages, gold

**Privilege**:
A small comfort the Worker can buy with Labour Points, such as a call home or a rest hour.
_Avoid_: reward, upgrade, item

**Gratitude**:
The App's parody premium currency. It is never bought with real money.
_Avoid_: gems, diamonds, premium currency
