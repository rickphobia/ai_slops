# Happy Cotton: story and endings (draft)

**Status:** draft. Decisions agreed with the owner (2026-10-08). It becomes a full spec with `/to-spec` after the economy spec (`economy.md`) and a Co-workers and Farm Levels spec, because two of the endings depend on them.

## Why endings in an endless game

The game stays endless by default: that is the Endless ending, and it never stops play. The other four are paths the player's choices lead to. Two of them are a moral fork: complicity (Promotion) or solidarity (Revolt). Neither path makes the Worker a villain on his own; when he becomes an enforcer, it is the system that makes him one. Poverty (Taken) and theft (Caught) pull against each other: keeping cotton back is how he pays for his Children, and it is what can get him taken away.

## The five endings

| Ending | Path | Trigger (recommended) | Last scene | Ends the game? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Taken** | Poverty | School fees unpaid three times in a row; the Children's letters stop first, as the warning | One of his Children on the Generator, the Overseer standing nearby. Never shown being punished | Yes: end card, then Start over |
| **Promotion** | Complicity | Quotas met and Reports filed on Co-workers, past a threshold | The state makes him Overseer; he holds the whip at the Generator while a Co-worker runs | Yes |
| **Revolt** | Solidarity | Enough Quiet Acts (covering Quotas, refusing to Report) that the Co-workers trust him | Together they stop the Generator for one day: the farm stands still and the loudspeaker is silent. Then the state replaces them all; the final card names each Co-worker | Yes |
| **Caught** | Theft | Caught keeping cotton back in a second Harmony Audit (the first takes his hidden savings and puts him on watch; see `land-and-hazards.md`) | He is taken away. The last scene is his empty spot on the track and a new Worker running there; The App calls it "reassignment". Shown as absence, nothing graphic | Yes |
| **Endless** | Default | None: what happens when no other ending does | A milestone card (for example at Shift 100, "The App thanks you for your service"), then play simply continues | No |

## Content notes

- The Children are never shown being punished (the hard line from planning). In Taken, a child at the Generator with the Overseer nearby is the whole scene.
- Student labour in the cotton harvest has been reported; the sources are checked when this becomes a full spec. If they don't hold up, Taken is marked as the game's own invention, like the Generator, the whip and the school fees.
- The Revolt is not a victory and not a tyranny: it is dignity with a cost. The Promotion is the system turning a victim into an enforcer, not the victim choosing cruelty.
